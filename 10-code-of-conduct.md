# All Day AI Code of Conduct

Version 1.0 | Last updated [date]

## Our pledge

All Day AI exists so that people building with AI inside real organizations can learn from each other. That only works if everyone in the room can participate without being harassed, belittled, or sold to.

We are committed to an event that respects the dignity, rights, and contributions of everyone taking part, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socioeconomic position, level of education, employer, seniority, or any other status. Everyone participating in good faith and in line with this Code of Conduct gets the same standing here, whether they are speaking on the main track or asking their first question in chat.

## Where this applies

This Code of Conduct covers every space connected to All Day AI:

- The event platform, including session chat, question and answer, polls, and any networking or breakout features
- The All Day AI Discord server, before, during, and after the event
- Speaker channels, tech checks, rehearsals, and organizer communication
- Official All Day AI social accounts and comment threads
- Any activity where someone is representing All Day AI publicly, including community partner events run under the All Day AI banner

It applies to attendees, speakers, MCs, moderators, producers, organizers, volunteers, and staff from supporting companies. Nobody's role exempts them, and that specifically includes people from the companies supporting the event.

## What we expect

Community norms differ, and text loses tone. Something written quickly at 2 AM in a second language can land very differently than it was meant to. With that in mind, we ask everyone to:

- Respect what this event is for and how people are using it
- Engage with others honestly and with some generosity
- Take other people's experience seriously, including when it does not match your own
- Own your words and actions, including the ones that did not land the way you intended
- Give and take feedback without making it personal
- Repair harm when you cause it, rather than defending the intent behind it
- Assume the person asking a basic question belongs here, because they do

## What is not acceptable

The following are violations of this Code of Conduct. So are threats to do them and encouragement of others to do them.

- **Harassment.** Continuing unwanted personal attention after being asked to stop, or ignoring a boundary someone has stated clearly.
- **Personal attacks.** Insulting, demeaning, or contemptuous remarks aimed at a person or a group.
- **Stereotyping and discrimination.** Characterizing someone's abilities, personality, or behavior on the basis of who they are.
- **Sexualized behavior.** Conduct or content that would be inappropriately intimate in a professional setting.
- **Breaching confidentiality.** Sharing or acting on someone's private information without permission. This includes screenshotting private messages and sharing details someone disclosed in a session that were clearly not meant to travel.
- **Endangerment.** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm.
- **Anything else that makes this a place people do not want to be.**

## Additional rules for this event

- **No sales pitches.** All Day AI does not run vendor content. Sessions cannot be pitches, and chat, question and answer, and Discord are not lead generation channels. Do not direct message attendees to sell them something. This rule applies to the companies supporting the event exactly as it applies to everyone else.
- **Credit your sources.** If you present someone else's work, data, code, or writing, say whose it is.
- **Do not misrepresent who you are.** No impersonating other people or organizations, and no returning under a new identity after an enforcement action.
- **Handle sensitive content responsibly.** If your session or comment needs to reference harmful material, including security research, present it with enough context that it informs people rather than harming them.
- **Respect other people's employers.** Many attendees are describing internal systems in public. Do not push a speaker or attendee to disclose more than they have chosen to share.

## Recording and privacy

Sessions are recorded and published after the event. Attendee cameras and microphones are not used, and questions or comments posted in session chat may appear in recordings and in content created from them.

Do not record, screenshot, or republish private conversations, direct messages, or Discord channels that are not public, and do not repost someone's contribution in a way designed to hold them up for ridicule.

## Reporting a problem

Not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct violation, and a lot of friction resolves itself. When something does need reporting, report it early rather than waiting to see if it gets worse.

You can report an issue in any of these ways:

- Email **[conduct@alldayai.[tld]]**, monitored by the organizing team
- Message any moderator in the All Day AI Discord. Moderators are marked with a distinct role
- Use the report function in the event platform, if available on the day
- Tell the MC or producer in a session, who will escalate immediately

On event day, October 6, reports go to a dedicated moderation channel staffed for the full program, and we aim to acknowledge them within 15 minutes and act during the event where action is needed. Outside the event, we aim to acknowledge reports within two business days.

Tell us what happened, where, and roughly when. Screenshots or links help but are not required. You can report on behalf of someone else, and you can report anonymously by email, though it limits our ability to follow up with you.

## How we handle reports

Moderators take reports seriously and investigate them. That can mean reviewing chat logs, session recordings, and Discord history, or speaking with the people involved and anyone who witnessed the incident.

We keep enforcement actions private between the people involved. We prioritize the safety and confidentiality of the person reporting, and we will not share their identity with the person reported without their agreement. Where a mutually agreed resolution includes telling the wider community something, we will.

If a moderator is involved in a report, they step out of handling it entirely.

## Consequences

Depending on what happened and its impact, we may use any of the following. For serious violations we skip straight to the response that fits, rather than working up from the bottom.

**Warning.** A private written warning explaining what crossed the line and what we expect going forward. Repair usually looks like a private apology and acknowledging the impact.

**Restricted participation.** A private warning plus a time-limited restriction, such as being muted in chat, removed from a Discord channel, or asked to stay out of a particular space for a period. Repair looks like using the time to reflect and re-entering carefully.

**Removal from the event.** Removal from a session or from the remainder of the event, with conditions attached to any future participation. Used for repeated violations after warnings, or for a single serious one.

**Permanent ban.** Removal from all All Day AI spaces, including Discord, with no return. Reserved for sustained patterns that other measures have not changed, or for behavior severe enough that the community cannot be kept safe otherwise.

Where a speaker violates this Code of Conduct, we may also stop their session, decline to publish the recording, or remove already published content.

This is a guideline, not a mechanical process. Moderators use judgment, and their responsibility is to the safety of the community.

## Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action against you was mistaken, email **[conduct@alldayai.[tld]]** within 30 days with a brief explanation. An organizer who was not involved in the original decision will review it. Appeal decisions are final.
